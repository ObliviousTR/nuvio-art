DC card (DC "peel" ident), rendered by nuvio-awards/art_tools/dc_peel.py:
  python3 art_tools/dc_peel.py <this _src dir> <out dir>
- The peel itself is modelled in the script from the measured geometry of the 2012 DC Entertainment logo (Landor).
- emb_*.png: the heroes' emblems, cut from each film's TMDB logo (Man of Steel 49521, Batman 268, Wonder Woman 297762,
  The Flash 298618); emb_lantern.png is modelled (the Green Lantern TMDB logo has its title across the emblem).
  They are shown in steel on the back of the peel.
- Montserrat (DC UNIVERSE / THE COLLECTION): SIL Open Font License, included.
