The mark is Pentagram's Wizarding World logo: a book whose fanned pages are nine wands, over the Harry Beast lettering.
--mode book (published): the cover is WIZARDING WORLD and THE COLLECTION alone, centred. On hover the lettering
  moves down, the book's line draws out from the spine and its wands turn over one by one like pages and stand in their
  fan; then the book closes (the wands fall back flat), the line draws back in and the lettering rises to the cover.
--mode map (kept, published before): the Marauder's Map: the mark is inked on blank parchment; the map's ink runs out
  from the mark through the castle plan; a pair of footprints walks round the mark and away; the ink withdraws.
