Wizarding World card, rendered by nuvio-awards/art_tools/wizarding_ident.py. Published: --mode map.
  python3 art_tools/wizarding_ident.py <this _src dir> <out dir> --mode map
The mark is Pentagram's Wizarding World logo: a book whose fanned pages are nine wands, over the Harry Beast lettering.
--mode map: the Marauder's Map: the mark is inked on blank parchment; the map's ink runs out from the mark through the
  castle plan (it keeps clear of the mark); a pair of footprints walks round the mark and away; the ink withdraws.
--mode book (kept, not published; Ozzy likes it, one small issue to fix later): the book opens: the wands fold onto
  the left page, then turn over one by one like pages and stand in their fan again.
- logo.jpg: the official gold Wizarding World logo on black (found via Pinterest), used for the gold and the wands.
- mark_flat.png: the same mark, flat in one colour (found via Pinterest), used as the ink in map mode.
- map.jpg: the Marauder's Map prop art (found via Pinterest); its castle plan (right of the title panel) is used.
- footprints.png: shoe prints (found via Pinterest).
- Cinzel (THE COLLECTION): SIL Open Font License, included.
- backdrop.jpg is unchanged. The earlier card had no script in the repos.
